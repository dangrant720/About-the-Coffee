Our Story

Start with coffee. See where it goes.

About the Coffee began with a simple habit: finding a coffee shop wherever we happened to be.

Over time, the stop became part of the adventure. A café before a hike. A roaster found on a road trip. A cup after a morning on the water. A small town we might have driven through if there weren't a coffee shop worth finding.

Traveling into the countryside of the Dominican Republic added another layer — seeing the land, people and process behind coffee and gaining a deeper respect for everything that happens before a cup reaches the table.

This is where we collect those experiences: the coffee, the people behind it, the places around it and whatever happens next.

The Philosophy

Coffee is life.

Not because we can't function without it. Because coffee starts with a living landscape and keeps creating reasons to gather, wander, learn, stop and notice where we are.
