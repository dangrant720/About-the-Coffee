Vivid Coffee

Burlington, Vermont
VISIT PHOTO — Vivid Coffee, Burlington

And so it begins. Back to where it all started—the trip that launched this journey for two coffee lovers. We returned to one of our favorite destinations, Burlington, Vermont, a city where coffee shops feel woven into the atmosphere. Our first stop in the About the Coffee log: Vivid Coffee.

OrderedLatte + Cortado
Take-Home BeansAbove Average
The Craft

Attention to the pull.

Even in the middle of a very busy shop, Vivid didn’t spare the details. Our drinks were built around a delicious, well-balanced espresso pull that left us both impressed. An abundance of brewing equipment, coffee gear, and Vivid swag reinforced the feeling that this wasn’t simply a café—it felt like a place serious about the craft and the baristas behind it.

The Place

Quick in. Good coffee. Keep moving.

Vivid worked well as a quick in-and-out stop and felt like a good place to get some work done. For us, it wasn’t quite the slower, unwind-and-stay-awhile atmosphere we were looking for.

What Made It Special

Burlington energy, balanced.

Vivid breathes in the hustle and bustle of Burlington while somehow balancing out the commotion—a coffee shop that feels connected to the energy of the city without being overwhelmed by it.

Take It Home

The beans.

None purchased this time.

The take-home coffee selection appeared priced above average.

Reflection

Where the story begins.

This journey had to begin with Vivid, but the story really starts with Burlington. For Dan and Becs, this city is part of the lineage of our love for finding great coffee. Hi—Dan, the author here. Long before About the Coffee, there was Burlington and our original favorite, Cosmic Dog Coffee. In a way, these coffee tours are an homage to where it all began.
